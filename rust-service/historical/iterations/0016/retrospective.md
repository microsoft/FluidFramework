## Practices to Keep, Change, or Stop

- **Keep:** exact contract text, practical owner-local diagnosis, bounded repair
	clusters, accepted adequate outcomes, direct Git-object review, and complete
	validation. Owners: quality workstream and coordinator.
- **Keep:** preserve unresolved inventory triggers rather than reopening them
	without new evidence. Owner: Phase 3 coordinator.
- **Keep:** reject summaries that omit enumerated source evidence. This is
	already enforced by coordination evidence-provenance guidance.
- **Stop:** unconditional full inventory reruns after this bounded repeat; use
	changed-boundary and concrete revisit triggers instead.
